# AI-Powered Lead Capture & Instant Qualification System

## Business Problem
Most service businesses lose qualified leads not because of poor marketing,
but because of slow response and zero qualification at the point of inquiry.
When a prospect fills a contact form, they typically wait hours for a reply,
with no way for the business to know if that lead is serious or just browsing.
Research shows leads contacted within 5 minutes are significantly more likely
to convert. Most businesses have no system handling that window.

## System Solution
This automation captures every form submission, runs it through an AI
qualification engine, routes it by lead quality, sends a personalized
acknowledgment email to qualified leads within seconds, logs every lead
to a tracker, and alerts the team with a full summary, all without any
manual input.

## Tools Used
- n8n (automation engine)
- Tally (form intake)
- Google Gemini (AI qualification)
- Gmail (automated follow-up email)
- Google Sheets (lead logging, HOT, WARM, and COLD tabs)
- Slack (team notification)

## How to Use This Workflow
1. Download the workflow.json file
2. Open n8n and go to Workflows
3. Click Import and select the file
4. Connect your Tally, Gmail, Google Sheets, Slack, and Gemini credentials
5. Update the Tally form ID to match your own form
6. Replace the booking link in the Gmail node with your actual calendar link
7. Activate the workflow

## Key Features
- AI scoring engine classifies every lead as HOT, WARM, or COLD
  based on budget and timeline
- HOT and WARM leads receive a personalized follow-up email within
  seconds of submitting
- All three lead types are logged to separate tabs in Google Sheets
  for easy pipeline tracking
- Team receives a Slack alert with the full lead summary and
  qualification score instantly
- Cold leads are captured and stored, not discarded

## Business Impact
- Zero manual lead checking required
- Sub-60-second response time to every qualified inquiry
- Sales team receives pre-qualified leads with context,
  not raw form data
- No hot lead missed because someone forgot to check a form
- Clean, organized lead data ready for CRM import or follow-up

## Known Limitations
- Qualification is based on form answers only,
  not behavioral or historical data
- No CRM integration in this version, leads are stored in Google Sheets
- AI scoring may misclassify edge cases if answers are vague or
  outside expected options
- No automated follow-up sequence for WARM leads that do not reply

## Planned Improvements
- Add Notion or Airtable CRM integration
- Build a follow-up sequence for WARM leads after 48 hours of no reply
