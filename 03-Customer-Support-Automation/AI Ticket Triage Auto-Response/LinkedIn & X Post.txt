Most businesses don't have a customer support problem.

They have a triage problem.

Every message lands in the same inbox. Every ticket competes for the same attention. And while your team is answering routine questions about gift wrapping and delivery times, a customer threatening to dispute a charge is still waiting.

I built a system that solves this completely.

Here is what happens the moment a customer submits a support ticket:
An AI agent reads the message and classifies it by category (billing, delivery, technical, general) and priority (urgent, normal, low). Urgent tickets trigger an immediate acknowledgment email to the customer and a detailed escalation alert to the support team on Slack with full context. Low and normal priority tickets receive an instant AI-generated resolution email with no human involvement required. Every ticket, regardless of priority, is logged to Google Sheets with the full details, AI classification, and timestamp.

The support team stops seeing every ticket. They only see the ones that actually need them.

That is the difference between a support inbox and a support system.

Built with: n8n, Tally, Google Gemini, Gmail, Slack, Google Sheets.
